Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions.

What are your live chat hours?

Our chat is available 24/7. Outside business hours you may be connected to our virtual assistant first, who can hand you over to an agent.

Do I need an account to chat?

No. You can start a chat as a guest — just share your name and email if you'd like a transcript.

Can I get a copy of my conversation?

Yes. At the end of the chat you can download or email yourself the transcript.

Is my information secure?

All conversations are encrypted and handled according to our privacy policy.
